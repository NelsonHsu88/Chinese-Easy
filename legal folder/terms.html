9.Learning content and automated features

Reference content can contain errors

The dictionary is built from CC-CEDICT, a community-maintained Chinese–English dictionary. Dictionary example sentences and their translations come from Tatoeba, a community-contributed sentence collection. Neither source is professionally verified. Definitions, readings, translations, character data and example sentences may be incomplete, outdated or wrong. Some example sentences are written by members of the public and may contain content that some people find inappropriate or offensive. We don't review every sentence individually.

Scores and schedules are estimates

Several features use automated, rule-based methods rather than human judgement:

  • Pronunciation practice uses your device's speech recognition service to work out what it heard, then scores the result syllable by syllable for initials, finals and tones. Recognition can be affected by background noise, microphones, accents and the recognition service itself, so a score may be higher or lower than your actual pronunciation deserves.
  • The review schedule estimates when you're likely to forget a word, using the FSRS algorithm and your answers.
  • The placement test estimates your starting level from a short sample of questions.
  • Read-aloud narration and word audio use your device's or browser's text-to-speech voices, which can mispronounce words or tones.

The Service does not use generative AI to create definitions, sentences, translations or feedback.

No guaranteed outcome

We don't guarantee that using the Service will bring you to any level of proficiency, or help you pass any exam, including the HSK. Levels shown in the app, such as "HSK 3", are guides to how difficult something is. They aren't official HSK assessments or certifications.

Microphone and speech

The app uses your microphone only when you start a pronunciation check. That audio is processed by your device's speech recognition service: Apple's on iOS, or Google's on Android. Depending on your device and settings, those companies may process the audio on their own servers under their own terms and privacy policies. You can decline microphone access and still use every other feature.

Assumed, needs confirming: operator name and whether it's an individual or a company, contact, security and copyright emails, mailing address, governing law (California assumed), venue, the US$50 liability floor, and 30 days' notice of changes. No arbitration clause or class-action waiver is included on purpose. Add one only on counsel's advice, because they bring their own notice and opt-out requirements.
  2. Subscriptions aren't live yet. No in-app purchase package is installed (src/lib/subscription.ts:9), and in a release build Subscribe says "Subscriptions aren't available in this build yet." Either connect a Store before launch or remove section 8 and the subscription lines in sections 3, 7 and 14. The app's reference prices are US$3/month and US$15/year (subscription.ts:186–204). They're kept out of the Terms on purpose, because the Store shows the real localised price.
  3. Age 13+ is the product decision recorded in src/lib/ads/adConfig.ts:105–110. The app collects no birth date, so the age requirement depends on the Terms alone. Counsel should confirm this is acceptable given the EEA's age of digital consent (up to 16) and the ads configuration (underAgeOfConsent: 'of-age').
  4. The progress disclaimer in section 5 depends on FEATURES.cloudSync being false (src/lib/features.ts:30–46). Update section 5 when syncing ships.
  5. Attribution gaps in the app. Settings → About credits CC-CEDICT, Unihan, hanzi-writer and the icon set (About.tsx:22–37), but not Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR), whose sentences ship in the word bank (scripts/buildWordBank.mjs:36–45). Add that credit. The hanzi-writer data is derived from Make Me a Hanzi, which is reportedly under the Arphic Public License rather than MIT. Confirm this and credit it correctly. The CC BY-SA ShareAlike obligation may also apply to the bundled dictionary data.
  6. Sign in with Apple token revocation. Account deletion removes the Supabase user (supabase/functions/delete-account/index.ts:65) but doesn't revoke the Apple token, which Apple's guidelines expect.
  7. Suspension (section 14) has no in-app mechanism. It relies on banning or deleting users in the Supabase dashboard. That works as a contractual right, but nothing automates it.
  8. The source repository (github.com/NelsonHsu88/Chinese-Easy) has no LICENSE file. If it's public, it's "all rights reserved" by default, which is consistent with section 11. If you publish it under an open-source licence, revise the reverse-engineering clause in section 6.
  9. Advertising details match adConfig.ts: banners on four screens, interstitials after a review session or story, G-rated content, UMP and ATT consent. Rewarded ads are built but not wired up (src/lib/ads/rewarded.ts:9), so the Terms don't mention them. Add terms if they launch.
