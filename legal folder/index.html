Remember what you learn

Chinese Easy brings each word back just before you'd forget it, using FSRS, a modern spaced-repetition scheduler. A few minutes a day keeps your whole vocabulary fresh.

Flashcards, listening, fill-the-gap and matching drills keep reviews from feeling like a chore.

Read stories made for your level

Folk tales, idiom stories and festival legends, written for every level from HSK 1 to HSK 6.

Tap any word to look it up or add it to your reviews. Or switch on audiobook mode and follow along as each story is read aloud.

Write it, then say it

Practise stroke order character by character, with a demonstration whenever you need one.

Then say the word out loud. Chinese Easy checks your pronunciation syllable by syllable, tones included.
