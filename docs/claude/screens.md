# Screens

Detail split out of CLAUDE.md. Read when working in this area.

## Screens — what to know

- **Launch intro** (`components/launch/LaunchIntro.tsx`, mounted by `LaunchLayer` in `_layout.tsx` *beside* `RootNavigator`): a pre-rendered video (`expo-video`, `surfaceType="textureView"`, no exo shutter) of Shifu assembled from silk and "Chinese Easy" handwritten. The real logo and wordmark are drawn underneath at the video's last-frame geometry (`lockup.ts`), so when the video ends it simply goes. Then the blue wave pushes the lockup off. The wave waits for the video **and** app readiness; every stage has a timed backstop (a video that never starts is skipped after 2.5s). A first launch's onboarding `Redirect` remounts the whole root layout, so progress and the `VideoPlayer` live at module level and a remount carries on. Animation endings are judged by elapsed time, because react-native-web reports a timely end as `finished: false`. Reduced motion skips the video: lockup, then a fade. The page, video background and native splash are all pure white, which survives the video's YUV round trip exactly.

- **Learning loader** (`components/loading/LearningLoader.tsx`): wraps an activity, shows Shifu writing (`ShifuWritingLoop`, a hardware-decoded video over its own first-frame still) and closes only when the activity calls `useLearningLoader().ready()` **and** `minDurationMs` (2s) has passed. If that lands mid-stroke with ≤450ms to go, it lets the stroke finish; then it fades for 280ms. `fail(e)`, or no answer in 20s, shows Retry/Back; Retry remounts the activity. Attempts are numbered, so stale answers are ignored. The activity mounts after `runAfterInteractions` (idle callback, with a backstop) plus one frame, so the loader paints first. **Loader animations pass `isInteraction: false`** — kept from when an endless loop counted as an interaction and blocked the mount forever. Frame waits go through `afterFrames` (timer backstop: hidden tabs never paint). Used by `ReviewSession` for `flashcards` and `mixed` (`LOADER_MESSAGES`): ready = plan built + the first writing card's stroke data loaded (`loadStrokeData`) + two frames. Reduced motion: the still, static dots. `useLearningLoaderGone()` is true once the overlay has fully gone (always true outside a loader) — anything audible on an activity's first screen waits for it (`ReviewListening`'s autoplay).
- **Completion celebration** (`components/celebration/LessonCompletionCelebration.tsx`, props `stats` + `onContinue`): Shifu crouches, jumps, turns about a third in perspective (a full 360° needs turnaround art — see `scripts/celebration/README.md`), lands with squash, gold marks and petals, then "Great job!", the result tiles and Continue. One clock (ms, native driver) drives everything through interpolations; feet stay planted under squash via a matching translate. Layout comes from window + insets (no `onLayout`): breathing room/jump headroom, stage, title, results + Continue; Shifu ≈ 42% of the content height, jump ≤ 100. A backstop forces the final frame; Continue goes live at 1.85s regardless of the clock. Reduced motion starts at the end. Fanfare + `successHaptic` on landing. Used by LessonPlayer's finish (review sessions end on `SessionEnd` instead). `/celebration-preview` is a `__DEV__`-only route (long-press Shifu to replay).
- **Home** (`Dashboard.tsx`, built to "SS 1012 New Home Redesigned UI"): `HomeHero` + `HomeCards` (Review and the word on offer side by side via `DashboardCard stretch`, then Daily Challenges and This Week), all Nunito, tokens in the `home*` section of `dashboard/tokens.ts`. Art is placed by the mockup's own crop boxes (`homeArt`/`homeRefCards` in `art.ts`) scaled to the real width — move a crop in the script and the box must move with it. The "Earn reward" pill sits on the patch in-painted out of the gift crop. Cards that hold their own link pills pass `accessibilityRole="none"` (nested `<button>` on web). Buttons are `LiftedFace` (shoulder + face). This Week uses calendar weeks (`currentWeekActivity`).
- **Onboarding** (`Onboarding.tsx`): pages from `STEP_ORDER`; welcome is built to "Onboarding New UI Redesign" (minus its app-mark tile, removed by request; `welcome-scene` — the reference's whole landscape with its painted title cleared by a hole — rising `WELCOME_SCENE_RISE` behind the subtitle); `pace` (after the goal) sets `dailyNewWordLimit` 1–50 on `WordsPerDayWheel` (PanResponder + `Animated.spring` carrying the release velocity, rubber-banded ends, tap a row to go to it; the value lives in the wheel and is reported only on settle so the page doesn't re-render per number; page `scroll={false}`); `OnbButton`/`OnbTextButton` play the tap sound (`sound={false}` where the press has its own); `OnbShell` = top bar / flexible middle / stable footer. Script page is second so `settings.script` exists before the placement test; the default is Simplified + pinyin (card shown first). **No Skip anywhere in onboarding** (removed by request; every page is answered). Before the test, `start` (dressed like the goal page: title, two `OnbChoiceCard`s, the second with a `badge`, over the `goal-branch`/`goal-foot` art) asks "Start from scratch" (no test → `ready` at HSK 1) or "Find my level" (→ `test`); the reminders page lands there. A retake from Settings still opens on the test. **An unfinished onboarding resumes where it was left**: `onboarding.progress` (page, back history, placement seed + answers) is saved as the learner goes and dropped by `completeOnboarding`; `profile` reopens on `account` (its form state is gone), and the "Log in" path saves nothing. Placement test is **adaptive** (`lib/placement.ts` `planPlacement`, replayed from the saved answers so it resumes): 3 HSK 1 warm-ups, then up a level on a right answer and down on a miss, at most `PLACEMENT_QUESTIONS` (15); a warm-up with nothing right ends it at HSK 1. Estimate = highest level with ≥2 right and ≥60% right (`estimatePlacement`). Words from `POOL_IDS` (by official HSK 3.0 level), split per seed into question words and wrong-answer words (`placementSplit`), so a wrong option is never a word the test could ask. Multiple choice (meaning/listening/cloze); **cloze shows no translation**; it deliberately keeps its classic card styling. `OnbPageTransition` mounts the arriving page underneath before the old one slides off; the spare slot needs `pointerEvents="none"`.
- **Challenges**: read targets via `challengeTarget`, lists via `offeredChallenges`. Claiming collapses and re-shows the card at `CLAIMED_OPACITY`. `charactersWritten`/`listeningAnswered` are device-only — `mergeDailyActivity` must carry them through.
- **Chinese Basics**: a list holding a sentence draws every row stacked (`listIsStacked`: hanzi across the row, a reading under each character via `RubySentence`, gloss beneath); sentence answer options go full-width, one per row. Zhuyin readings are centred on their glyph with the tone mark hanging (`SyllableText`; headwords use `RubyWord`) — split readings on `/\s+/`, since zhuyin syllables are joined by an en space. The sentence builder's tiles fly between bank and line (`motion/flipTiles.ts`, FLIP measured with `getBoundingClientRect` in a layout effect, ids never indices); each word keeps a fixed bank slot, a hole while placed.
- **Level-up test** (`/level-test`, `screens/LevelTest.tsx`, rules in `lib/levelTest.ts`): from Progress and from Profile's "Take the HSK N test" button under HSK Journey (the Profile screen guide spotlights both, between Scholar’s Garden and the Achievements row); hidden at HSK 6. Twenty words of the next level in Mixed Drills' listening/gap screens, three hearts (`DrillHeaderExtra` context), "I don't know" counts as a miss; passing raises `hskLevel`. Never touches the SRS deck.
- **New Words**: no counter or progress bar (by request): the gold "Daily goal reached" note fades in and out each time the screen opens with the goal met, and when an Add reaches it. Practice writing is a mint secondary button under the solid-green Add. Swipe left = skip, right = previous; neighbours pre-mounted and hidden by a wrapper's opacity; ignore taps for 80ms after a drag. No custom-word entry in the UI any more.
- **Dictionary**: search defaults to English; an English | Pinyin switch sits inside the search field (`SearchModeToggle`), and when English finds nothing for Latin letters the pinyin matches are shown with a note. Typed Hanzi always search every field. Built to its Beta 2.1 reference without brand block; hairlines, no shadows; Word to discover is `wordToDiscover`. Explore tiles: HSK Vocabulary, **Recent** (opens the recents shelf; replaced Characters), Radicals, My Words — no Recent list on the landing. Its raised `Pill`, `FilterButton` and `FilterPanelCard` are shared with Books' shelf filter. Haptics on release when something changed; sound only for adding a word. Result cards carry no example sentence. The word page's pull-up sheet (`InfoSheet`) has **no back or close buttons** (removed by request): it closes by swiping down, tapping its handle, Escape on web, or Android Back. `/radicals` (`Radicals.tsx`, `radicals/tokens.ts`) wears the My Words header (bonsai corner art, pill search) with cream tiles grouped by stroke count. Opening a result is a plain push with the stack's fade-up (an "ink" shared-element transition was tried and removed 2026-10-25: JS-driven layout animation over a heavy page mount never ran smoothly in development builds).
- **Profile / Settings**: Profile is the fifth tab (`/profile-tab`; `/profile` and `/profile/edit` redirect there). Settings is a standalone page at `/settings` (`app/settings/index.tsx`, back arrow to Profile) — a hub + seven screens under `src/screens/settings/`. **`settings/developer` is `__DEV__`-only in two places** (hidden row in `General.tsx` *and* a `Redirect` guard above every hook in `Developer.tsx`) — its controls rewrite progress and entitlement. Nothing requests a native permission on mount.
- **Subscription**: no IAP package; `lib/subscription.ts` takes a `registerStore(adapter)`. Never grant on a button press — only from the store's outcome. `isAdFree` is the single source of truth (no `isPremium`).
- **Tour** (`components/tour/`, `lib/tour.ts`): four steps, offered not imposed, never resumed mid-step; spotlight is a wide border, targets via `<TourTarget id>`. A learner placed at HSK 1 (`tourIncludesBasics`, fixed at `startTour` as `tour.withBasics`) gets a fifth step on the Learn tab pointing at Chinese Basics; reaching `/basics` ends the tour and marks the Learn screen guide seen.
- **Books / reader**: `/books` is the Reading Corner (`Books.tsx`, SS1026): Continue card (follows `settings.lastStoryId`, set once the reader is past the cover; falls to the next unfinished story at the learner's level), Explore (feature + two, illustrated stories first), three chips grouping the five collections (Everyday / Culture = festivals+chengyu / Tales = folk tales+myths) filtering a Recommended row. See all / More stories open `/stories` (`AllStories.tsx`: the three level shelves). Covers: `reading/storyCovers.ts` (per-story, from `processReadingCornerArt.mjs`; all 46 stories have a painting; a new story without one wears the general cover); on web an `Image` needs explicit width/height or it renders at the file's pixel size. `BookCover` fades in once room and cover images load. Reader is `PageFlip` pages, each with its own turn value bound once; turn decided by distance (`TURN_DISTANCE`) or flick; PanResponder zeroes `dx` at grant, so use `claimedDx`. Narration (`lib/narration.ts`) rebuilds a timeline from `onBoundary` offsets; prefer local voices (Chrome remote voices fire no boundaries). Pagination (`lib/pagination.ts`) packs whole sentences; the book passes its own budget.
- **"I don't know"** is the same quiet raised button everywhere (drills' `AnswerButtons` look; flashcard beside Show Answer; Match the Pairs beside Continue). In the drills it re-queues ungraded but calls `markWordUnknown` (bumps `recentLapses`), so the word shows under Weak words and in Mistakes; a flashcard's is an Again.
- **Speaking practice**: a Mixed Drills step (`kind: 'speaking'`, from `buildMixedSteps({ speaking })`), never after a flashcard. It follows the first question and every `SPEAK_EVERY`th one after it, on that question's word, at most `MAX_SPEAKING` per session, and only where `isSpeechRecognitionAvailable()`. Ungraded, like match. `PronunciationCheck` wears the plain `DrillShell` header. "I can't speak now" drops the remaining speaking steps (`withoutSpeaking`). Mic is a raised `LiftedFace` disc centred between card and buttons. `gradePronunciation` compares syllables (initial 0.45 / final 0.40 / tone 0.15, `PASS_THRESHOLD` 0.7) — sound, not spelling.
- **Example sentences** render through `ReadingSentence.tsx` (per-word readings; `settings.showReadings` fades them without moving layout).
