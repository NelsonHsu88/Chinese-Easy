# Asset pipeline

Detail split out of CLAUDE.md. Read when working in this area.

### Asset pipeline (`scripts/`)

Plain `node scripts/<name>.mjs`, run by hand; outputs are committed. Most read from the gitignored `Photos for Reference/` (~225MB source art), so they can't run on a fresh clone.

- **Art croppers** (`process*Art.mjs`: Home, Learn, NewWords, Library, Settings, Review, MyWords, Challenges, Listening, Flashcard, SessionEnd, OnboardingRedesign) cut painting out of Beta 2.1 mockups and key it into alpha against the surface it sits on. Each **checks the mockup's pixel size** (usually 941×1672; Flashcard 705×1179; Learn 995×1580; Home 863×1822) and keys against colours sampled from it. **Page backgrounds are pure white by request** (every screen's `background`/`page` token, `canvas`, `dict.page`, `read.cream`) — the art was keyed against near-white cream, so it sits on white without a visible halo. Card fills the art sits on (`homeSurfaces`, `revColors.card`, `dashColors.card`, `fcColors.card`, `listenColors.*` cards) **must stay exactly as sampled**, or re-run the script. `processHomeArt.mjs` also diffuses text and controls out of its crops (`inpaint`), so the screen can draw its own on top.
- **Trim-and-resize** (`processDashboardArt`, `processOnboardingArt`, `processSubscriptionArt`, `processTourArt`): sources carry real alpha; the trim matters because art is positioned against card edges and a transparent margin silently becomes an offset. Tour checks its sheet is 1536×1024; `components/tour/art.ts` and `RedesignArt.tsx` carry asset sizes to update on re-run.
- `processReadingCornerArt.mjs` — per-story covers (`library/covers/<id>.jpg`, 540×720, map in the script; regenerate `reading/storyCovers.ts` if the set changes) , the Reading Corner chip icons and its header painting (`corner-header.png`: cloud, range, pagoda, sakura, with the mockup's "Corner" and streak pill painted out) from SS1026 (881×1784).
- `processLaunchArt.mjs` — the launch intro's logo (`src/assets/images/launch/shifu-logo.png`, 720px) from `assets/images/icon.png`; checks the icon's background is pure white, which the intro's `launchColors.page` must match.
- `scripts/launch/` (see its README) → `src/assets/video/launch-intro.mp4` + `components/launch/lockup.ts` (generated). `layers.mjs` splits the flat icon into animatable layers by colour + region, `handwriting.mjs` reduces Caveat Bold "Chinese Easy" to pen-stroke centre lines, `scene.mjs` is the choreography (time → SVG), `render.mjs` rasterises with sharp and encodes with ffmpeg (needs it on PATH). Re-run after changing any of them or the icon.
- `scripts/loading/` (see its README) → `src/assets/video/loading-shifu.mp4`, `src/assets/images/loading/shifu-writing-still.jpg`, `components/loading/loop.ts` (generated): the learning loader's seamless 3.2s loop of Shifu writing, built from the flat painting `scripts/loading/shifu-writing.webp` (hand + brush cut out, the hole in-painted, the brush tip's path designed and the hand solved from it).
- `scripts/celebration/cutout.mjs` (see its README) → `src/assets/images/celebration/shifu-jump.png`: the jumping Shifu keyed out of `great-job-reference.webp` (paper-distance alpha inside a traced outline, then a closed, hole-filled solid silhouette). Update `SHIFU_ART` in `components/celebration/tokens.ts` if it changes size.
- `processAppIcon.mjs` — app icon set (`assets/images/icon.png`, adaptive foreground/background/monochrome, favicon) from `SS 1012 New App Icon.png`.
- `compressImages.mjs` — palette-quantises every PNG under `src/assets/images` (24.8MB → 6.3MB). **Re-run after any art script.**
- `buildWordBank.mjs` → both word tiers from CC-CEDICT in `.wordbank-data/`; **always follow with `npx tsx scripts/repairExampleSenses.mjs`** (tsx: it reuses the app's gloss logic; see Word data). It picks each form's CC-CEDICT reading via `scripts/lib/cedict.mjs` `chooseRow` (single characters take the Unihan common reading, with a hand-checked `KEEP_FIRST_ROW` list); `fixEntryReadings.mjs` applies that choice to the committed bank without a rebuild; it skips entries whose right row has another traditional form, which `fixTraditionalForms.mjs` fixes (几→幾, 叶→葉, 价→價, 于→於…; also in `KEEP_TRADITIONAL`). `cleanGloss` **keeps notes that narrow a meaning** (喝酒 "to drink (alcohol)", "(Taiwan) hostess club") and up to six senses; `node scripts/refreshGlosses.mjs` re-applies it to both tiers in place. `npx tsx scripts/addMissingWords.ts [--dry]` puts back words an old builder dropped (船, 笑, 最… — 208 added 2026-10-24) at their frequency position without touching any other entry; they carry no example sentence until a full rebuild with `cmn.txt`. `buildHanziData.mjs` (stroke data, tier 1, both scripts), `buildRadicalIndex.mjs` and `buildCharacterPinyin.mjs` (Unihan) — re-run when the bank grows.
- `applyHskLevels.mjs` — sets every bank entry's `hskLevel`/`hskOfficial` from `scripts/data/hsk30Levels.json` (HSK 3.0, MIT, from github.com/drkameleon/complete-hsk-vocabulary). **Re-run after `buildWordBank.mjs`**, which writes frequency bands.
- `generateSounds.mjs` (WAV effects), `embedHanziWriterEngine.mjs` (after bumping `hanzi-writer`). `mergeHanziData`, `processReviewIcons`, `buildStoryCovers` are superseded/unused.
- `assertBuildEnv.mjs` runs as `eas-build-pre-install`: refuses a preview/production build with missing `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_*` or any `EXPO_PUBLIC_*` that looks like a `service_role` key. The runtime check in `lib/supabase.ts` is deliberately soft (signed-out, not crash), which is wrong for a release.
